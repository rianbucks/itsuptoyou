A Fictional Chronicle of Varnia (fictional modern study)

ch.1
In 40 BC the kingdom of Arden seized the ferry crossing on the border river. Two years later, in 38 BC, Varnia and Arden agreed to a truce that made the river the boundary.

ch.2
A later anecdote, unsupported by any surviving annal, places his first schooling around 33 BC.

ch.3
In 20 BC the Varnian senate reformed the coinage, fixing a new weight for the silver piece.

ch.4
The expedition against the pirate island ended with a frontal assault that took the island. Coastal villages near the island suffered in the fighting.

ch.9
In 1 BC he concluded an alliance treaty with the northern tribal league. The treaty was signed at the border fortress, although another tradition quoted here places the meeting at Portus Novus.

ch.15
He died at his house in Portus Novus in AD 20. The annals record an illness; this study also reports a story, current among his political rivals' opponents, that he was poisoned.
